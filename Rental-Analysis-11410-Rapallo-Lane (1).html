Why a Range, Not a Single Number

Windermere is one of Central Florida's most desirable enclaves, built around the Butler Chain of Lakes — and its homes are wonderfully non-cookie-cutter. That individuality is part of the charm, but it also means clean rental comparables are scarce; no two homes are quite alike, so the market itself becomes the truest appraiser. For a home like yours, the wisest approach is to launch at a well-supported, proven number and let real showing activity tell us whether there's room to move up.

The Nuances Worth Naming

A market that prices itself

With so many custom and semi-custom homes around the lakes, true comps are rare. Launching at a defensible number and reading live feedback is more reliable than forcing a comp-driven price.

Condition meets the market

Your home shows well with neutral, move-in-ready interiors — a real advantage. In a luxury-leaning market, pricing is calibrated to how the home presents today against its competition, which is exactly what the recommended figure reflects.

Honest expectation-setting

This is a special home in a coveted location, and it deserves the right tenant at the right price. Pricing realistically from day one is what protects you from a long, costly vacancy.

How We Work — Vacant & Show-Ready

To deliver the best-qualified tenant at the strongest rent, your home needs to be fully vacant and show-ready before we bring it to market. This is our standard on every home — and it's our standard for good reasons.

  • Well-qualified renters want to see exactly what they'll move into — clean, empty, and ready for their own life. An occupied home, however lovely, makes that hard to picture, weakens the photography, and quietly turns away the strongest applicants.
  • It lets us say yes to the right tenant's timing. When a wonderful prospect wants to move in within two weeks, a vacant-and-ready home means we can accommodate them. If the home is still occupied, we can't — and we risk losing the very renter we worked so hard to attract.
  • Fewer moving parts, less downside. Showing a vacant, prepared home removes the friction, scheduling conflicts, and timing mismatches that slow a lease and weaken your position. This is the part you're entrusting to us — the judgment to set things up so the fewest things get in the way of a clean, fast, high-quality lease.

We completely understand if vacating first isn't workable for everyone. It is simply how we list — and it's a standard we hold because it consistently protects your outcome.

The Question for You

Because your home is currently occupied, the deciding factor is simply this: are you in a position to fully vacate and present the home empty and show-ready before we market it? If yes, we'd be honored to go to work. If the timing isn't right just yet, that's perfectly alright — we're happy to revisit whenever it is.
